**Name (30):** BP Log: Blood Pressure Tracker
**Subtitle (30):** Log, chart & share with doctor

**Promo text:** Two taps to log a reading. One tap to hand your doctor a clean PDF. Free, private, no account.

**Description**

Doctors ask for the same thing: log your blood pressure twice a day and bring it in. BP Log makes that easy.

- Log in two taps. Big numbers, number pad, done. Or point the camera at your cuff and let it read the numbers.
- Bluetooth cuffs: Omron, A&D, Beurer, Withings, iHealth and any cuff using the standard Bluetooth blood pressure profile. No pairing app.
- See where you stand right away: normal, elevated or high. Pick the guideline you and your doctor use: AHA (US), ESC (Europe), WHO, JNC 7, or set your doctor's target.
- Medications: your list, one tap when you take them, and every reading tagged before or after meds.
- Tags for context: after exercise, stressed, sitting or lying down, irregular heartbeat, after coffee.
- Family profiles for a spouse or parent. Free, no limit.
- 7 and 30 day averages, morning vs evening, and a trend chart.
- Doctor report: a clean PDF with averages, ranges and every reading. Share it from your phone.
- Apple Health sync for blood pressure, pulse, blood oxygen and weight. ECG and irregular rhythm alerts from Apple Watch shown next to your readings.
- Apple Watch app: log from your wrist, see your latest reading on the watch face.
- Home screen widget and Siri: "Log my blood pressure in BP Log."
- Export as PDF or spreadsheet (CSV), email it straight to your doctor.
- Face ID lock.
- Reminders for your morning and evening readings.
- Private by design. No account. Your data stays on your phone and your iCloud.

Free with a small banner ad. Remove the ads for $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year. Every feature is free either way.

BP Log is a logbook. It does not measure blood pressure (you need a cuff for that) and does not diagnose or treat anything. Talk to your doctor about what your numbers mean.
