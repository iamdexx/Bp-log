**Description**

Doctors ask for the same thing: log your blood pressure twice a day and bring it in. BP Log makes that easy.

- Log in two taps. Big numbers, number pad, done.
- See where you stand right away: normal, elevated or high, using the American Heart Association ranges.
- 7 and 30 day averages, morning vs evening, and a trend chart.
- Doctor report: a clean PDF with averages, ranges and every reading. Share it from your phone.
- Apple Health sync for blood pressure, pulse, blood oxygen and weight.
- Reminders for your morning and evening readings.
- Private by design. No account. Your data stays on your phone and your iCloud.

Free with a small banner ad. Remove the ads for $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year. Every feature is free either way.

BP Log is a logbook. It does not measure blood pressure (you need a cuff for that) and does not diagnose or treat anything. Talk to your doctor about what your numbers mean.
